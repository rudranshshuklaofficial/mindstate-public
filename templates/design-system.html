Type & spacing

Google Sans Flex for interface and body text; Instrument Serif for editorial moments. Use 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 and 48px spacing. Keep body text readable, actions generously padded, and focus indicators visible.

Build from shared patterns

Post cards: image, category labels, title, date and author. Forms: visible labels, helpful descriptions, inline errors and a clear save action. Reading pages: two distinct leaves with content kept clear of the binding. Reviews: evidence on the left, text and decisions on the right.
